Farmkart
Live demo of a university project. Browse and fill the cart freely; checkout is simulated and no payment or personal data is collected.

Everything a farm needs, in one place

Farmkart is an online store for farming essentials: seeds, flowering plants, fruit plants, tools and pest control, with guidance on what to sow each season. Built as a full-stack PHP and MySQL web app; this page is its live catalogue.

🌱 What to sow this season

Seasonal guidance from the store's product data: pick a season to see what to plant.

About the project

Built for CSE3002 Internet & Web Programming at VIT (Fall 2023–24) by Biggyat Kumar Pandey and Pawar Adwyait Shivaji. The goal: a gateway where farmers can buy everything they need without depending on middlemen.

Original features

  • User registration, login and profile (PHP sessions, MySQL)
  • Category pages with pagination, search and stock tracking
  • Cart, payment gateway page and order history
  • Contact form with captcha; seasonal product guidance
